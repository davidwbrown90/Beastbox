Privacy Policy — Beast Box

Last updated: August 2026

Beast Box does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no network requests of any kind.
  • High scores are saved only on your device, using standard local storage. They are never sent anywhere and are deleted if you uninstall the app.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking software is included.
  • No third parties receive any information from this app, because none is collected in the first place.

If this policy ever changes, an updated version will be posted at this same address.
